How we bring the cost down

The offer does the work, not the budget

In candidate marketing the creative and the offer decide the cost per application far more than targeting. Most of our results come from testing offers, not from bidding harder.

01

Offer testing

Visa support, free registration, guaranteed hours, housing, paid training. We test which promise moves the cost per application in your market — the winning offers cut cost per lead 2–3×.

02

Creative volume

Static, carousel and short vertical video, localized per geo and language. 8–12 new creatives a month, so the algorithm always has something fresh to spend on.

03

Quality feedback loop

Your recruiters mark which applicants showed up and were hired. That feedback goes back into the campaigns — otherwise you optimise for people who never answer the phone.
